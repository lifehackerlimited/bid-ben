Large contracts: expect TUPE and pension costs

Worth it if you've run contracts this size before. Price any inherited staff carefully: it's where most bids go wrong.

Cleaning Services Contract for Orbital South Colleges: East Surrey College and John Ruskin College Small-firm fit: low

East Surrey College · Redhill, Surrey

Value: £2m over 2 years (about £1m a year)Ask to take part by: Fri 27 Nov, 12:00

Orbital South Colleges (OSC) is a leading further education group comprising East Surrey College, John Ruskin College, Reigate School of Art and Orbital South Colleges University Centre.

Watch out: A large contract. Expect a full tender pack with scored written answers. The notice mentions staff transferring to the winner (TUPE) or a pension scheme, so price the inherited wages and pension costs before anything else. It's a staged process, usually starting with a short questionnaire.

How to bid: through panacheconsultancy.co.uk; contact tenders@esc.ac.uk.
