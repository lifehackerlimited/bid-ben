How we use your details

The short version: we use your email to send you Bid Ben, and for nothing else.

Who we are

Bid Ben is run by Betttech Global Ventures Ltd, company no. 11753604, registered office 7 Clim Down, Kingsdown, Deal, Kent CT14 8EX. We are the controller of the information described here. Contact us at info@betttech.co.uk.

What we collect, and why

When you sign up we collect your email address and, if you give them, your company name, your trade and the areas you cover. We use them to send you the weekly email and to put the tenders nearest you first. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give by signing up and can withdraw at any time.

Our form provider, Formspree, processes the sign-up form and passes it to us by email. The weekly emails are sent from our own mailbox.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your details, and we don't add you to any other list. This site sets no cookies and has no advertising or tracking scripts.

Stopping, and your rights

Reply "unsubscribe" to any email, or write to info@betttech.co.uk, and we'll stop and delete your details. Otherwise we keep them for as long as you're subscribed, and for up to a year after a free trial ends in case you come back. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold at any time, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

The tender information

Names and contact details of buyers' staff on these pages come from the official notices, which buyers publish so that firms can bid. If you're named and would like it removed here, email us.
